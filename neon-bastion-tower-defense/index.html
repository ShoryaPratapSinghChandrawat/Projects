HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Enemies march along the glowing path towards your core. Every enemy that reaches it costs lives. Survive all waves to win the sector.

Controls

  • 1-6 or click a tower in the shop to pick it, then click a free tile to build
  • Shift + click keeps building the same tower
  • Click a tower to select it: U upgrade, S sell, T change targeting
  • Space start / call next wave (early = bonus gold)
  • F game speed   A auto-wave   P / Esc pause   Right-click cancels

Tips

  • Build near corners, where towers can cover the path twice.
  • Mix damage types: splash for swarms, Tesla against shields, Rail against armor.
  • Every 10th wave a Titan boss appears. Be ready!
