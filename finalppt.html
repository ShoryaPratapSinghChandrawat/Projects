Why it happens

Each system is right.
Together, they’re blind.

Faculty cancels a class — and can’t see who is already travelling.

Transport runs on schedule — and can’t see the class was cancelled.

Student decides alone — with half the picture.

Root cause

Data silos, not data scarcity.

🧩

Fragmentation

Every department runs its own database. Nothing cross-checks anything.

🕒

Delay

Updates are manual, so news is old by the time students see it.

🔍

No single place to ask

You must know which system to open. There is no one interface.

The failure is architectural: no shared schema, no query layer, no real-time sync.

Our inspiration

God's Eye View: from planet to campus.

Privacy & honesty

Useful, and clearly a simulation.

🚫

No tracking

Synthetic agents only. No real student location, ever.

🏷️

Everything labelled

Only the timetable and the story are real. Routes, people and metrics are marked simulated.

✋

Human in the loop

Notices are drafts. Faculty reviews before anything is sent. Nothing is auto-sent.

🔑

Key stays local

Gemini key lives in your browser tab; Cloud Run proxy planned for deployment.
