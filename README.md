## How to explore

1. Open a project link to read its own README, setup instructions, and verification notes.
2. Check the documented limitations before interpreting results or assuming a live integration.
3. Some projects are prototypes, simulations, or learning experiments rather than production services.

## Working principles

- **Reproducibility:** include setup steps and scripts so others can inspect or rerun the work.
- **Verification:** distinguish local tests, CI results, simulations, and live integrations.
- **Honest scope:** document limitations rather than implying unverified capabilities.
- **Learning by building:** use coursework, personal experiments, and team projects to strengthen engineering fundamentals.
