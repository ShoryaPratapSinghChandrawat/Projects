### [QFF 2026 — Quantum VQE Simulation](https://github.com/ShoryaPratapSinghChandrawat/qff2026-phase1-s1-chicken-leg-piece)
A team project for Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 exploring a Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) approach to the ground state of a four-site transverse-field Ising model.

- **Stack:** Python, Qiskit, quantum-circuit simulation, numerical optimisation
- **Focus:** Hamiltonian construction, VQE experiments, circuit compilation, and comparisons across processor-connectivity/noise models
- **Project status:** Research and simulation project. Results should be interpreted within the assumptions and limitations documented in the repository; no claim of quantum advantage is made.

### [FlipTrace — Decision Reversal Pattern Detector](https://github.com/ShoryaPratapSinghChandrawat/Projects/blob/main/Readmefordetecting%20decisions%20reversal%20patterns.md)
A browser-based tool that looks for changes and reversals in decisions across exported workplace-style conversations and task data.

- **Stack:** JavaScript, browser APIs, Web Workers, IndexedDB/localStorage
- **Work includes:** Importing supported text/JSON/CSV formats, phrase-based extraction, similarity-based grouping, reversal-pattern detection, and timeline/dashboard views
- **Approach:** Rule-based and text-similarity methods, not a deep-learning or LLM system.
