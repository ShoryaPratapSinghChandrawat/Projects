Why we built this

A morning class was cancelled, and I found out only when a friend called. I’m a hosteller, but day scholars living 20–30 km away can spend over an hour travelling for nothing. Campus schedules, transport, notices and facilities reports may sit in separate systems. Weave explores connecting those signals so the impact is visible before people leave.

Students: earlier, relevant updates. Faculty: a clearer view of who may be affected. Campus leaders: a proposed way to measure preventable journeys—after validation, not as a claim about current SRM statistics.

Simulation, privacy & assumptions

Only the Monday–Saturday timetable and the personal cancellation story are user-provided. Routes, stop sequences/times, profiles, attendance, deadlines, agent locations, pain ranking, event ripple, and semester metrics are fictional/simulated. The attendance threshold (75%), distance weights, fares (₹2.5/km) and map positions are editable in CONFIG. This does not track real people or access university systems.
